Privacy Policy

We believe convenience should come with clarity about how your information is handled.

Effective date: 5 November 2026
Website: https://iraretreats.com
Organisation: IRA — Instant Retail Access LLP, Madurai, Tamil Nadu, India.

1. Information we collect

When you contact IRA, suggest a location or product, submit a partnership enquiry, or send feedback, we may receive the information you choose to provide. This can include your name, email, phone number, organisation or location details, suggestions, and the contents of your message. Some forms may be handled through Google Forms or other services identified when you submit them.

2. Website analytics and cookies

With your permission, we use Google Analytics 4 to understand website usage, such as page visits, general traffic sources, device/browser information and interactions. Google Analytics may use cookies and similar technologies and process technical information, including online identifiers and approximate location derived from network information. We do not intentionally send names, email addresses, phone numbers, UPI references or message contents to Google Analytics.

Our site asks you to accept or reject optional analytics before the Google Analytics script is loaded. You can revisit your choice using the Cookie preferences link in our website footer. Rejecting analytics does not prevent you from using the website.

3. Why we use information

We use submitted information to respond to enquiries, evaluate proposed vending locations and partnerships, understand product preferences, provide customer support, address feedback, maintain site security, and improve IRA's services and website. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Services and sharing

Information may be processed by service providers we use for website hosting, forms, email and analytics, including GitHub Pages and Google services, subject to their applicable terms and privacy policies. We may also disclose information where legally required. Information processed by these providers may be stored or accessed outside India.

5. Retention and security

We retain enquiry and support information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose collected, business records, dispute handling or applicable legal obligations. We use reasonable safeguards, but no internet transmission or storage system is completely secure. Analytics retention is managed through our Google Analytics property settings.

6. Your choices and requests

You may ask us about information you provided, request correction or deletion where applicable, or withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. Email us at connect@iraretreats.com. You can also reject or revisit optional analytics through the cookie controls on our website. Browser settings may let you manage other cookies.

7. Third-party links

Our website may link to Instagram, Google Forms and other third-party websites. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

8. Children's information

Our website is intended for general audiences and business enquiries. We do not knowingly seek personal information from children without appropriate authorisation. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information requiring review.

9. Updates and contact

We may update this policy as our website, forms and services change. Material changes will be reflected on this page. For privacy questions, contact connect@iraretreats.com.
