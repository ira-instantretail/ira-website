Good
questions.

Straight answers about where we are today.

What is IRA?

IRA — Instant Retail Access — is a smart vending concept focused initially on packaged snacks and beverages.

Where is IRA starting?

IRA’s first machine launch is planned for Madurai in January 2027.

What products will be available?

The initial focus is packaged snacks and beverages. The final assortment will depend on customer research and location needs.

Who can partner with IRA?

Colleges, universities, hospitals, hostels, offices, gyms, turfs and other suitable locations in Madurai can enquire.

How does payment work?

IRA’s planned machines support digital payment through UPI / QR and touchscreen product selection.

Is the machine refrigerated?

Yes. Refrigeration is part of the planned vending-machine specification for appropriate products.

Does IRA operate 24/7?

The machine is designed for 24/7 operation, subject to the host location’s access rules, power and operating arrangements.

Are you expanding to other categories?

Potential future categories are being considered, but they are not part of the initial launch offering.

What does IRA manage at a partner location?

IRA plans to coordinate product selection, restocking and machine servicing. Site access, utilities, commercials and responsibilities are agreed with each partner before installation.

Is the IRA Fit Check an approval or revenue estimate?

No. It is a preliminary guide. Suitability depends on actual demand, power, site access, space, service routes and mutually agreed terms.
